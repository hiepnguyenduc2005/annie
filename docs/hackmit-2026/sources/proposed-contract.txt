dog.status — Body → all, on change
{"ts":0,"state":"idle|patrolling|paused|estop|lowbatt","battery_pct":0,"waypoint":"kitchen","pose":{"x":0,"y":0,"yaw":0}}

dog.map — Body → App, on map update (every 30 s)

dog.cmd — Brain/App → Body
{"ts":0,"cmd":"goto|stop|resume|look","waypoint":"kitchen","pose":{"x":0,"y":0}}

brain.perception — Brain → all, per tick
{"ts":0,"frame_id":"uuid","person":true,"posture":"standing|sitting|lying","location":"bed|floor|chair|unknown","confidence":0.0,"caption":"...","pose":{"x":0,"y":0}}

brain.event — Brain → App/Voice
{"ts":0,"event_id":"uuid","kind":"checkin_ok|checkin_no_reply|fall_suspected|fall_confirmed|reminder_due","evidence":{"frame_id":"uuid","crop_b64":"...","pose":{"x":0,"y":0}},"severity":"info|warn|critical"}

voice.say — Brain/App → Voice
{"ts":0,"text":"...","priority":"critical|info","cache_key":"are_you_ok|null"}

voice.heard — Voice → Brain/App
{"ts":0,"text":"...","confidence":0.0,"speaker":"resident"}

memory.write — Brain → Memory service
{"ts":0,"frame_id":"uuid","caption":"...","entities":["person","cup"],"pose":{"x":0,"y":0},"room":"kitchen"}

Alert path: fall_suspected → Voice says are_you_ok → 8 s window for voice.heard → no reply or "help" → fall_confirmed → App card + Linq SMS to family with crop + map pin + POST /say shortcut.
Debounce rules (Brain-owned): lying + location != bed on two consecutive ticks → fall_suspected. VLM timeout > 3 s → skip tick, never alert on a timeout.
